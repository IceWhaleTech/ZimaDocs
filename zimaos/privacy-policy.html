Privacy Policy

Last updated July 12, 2024

This privacy notice describes how IceWhale Technology Limited (“Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) processes your information when you use ZimaOS and related services.

If you have questions or do not agree with these practices, please discontinue use and contact us at support@icewhale.org.

At a Glance

Privacy Aspect Our Commitment
Personal data collection We do not collect, store, or analyze your personal information
Sensitive information We do not process any sensitive personal data
Data storage We do not store any of your data in any form
Data sharing / selling We do not sell user data or engage in any data-brokering activity
Information we receive Device information and encrypted data — used only to deliver services

What Information Does ZimaOS Collect?

We only receive your device information and encrypted data to provide Services. We do not collect, store, use, or analyze your personal information or data.

The following table outlines common data categories and whether we collect them:

Category Examples Collected?
Identifiers Name, alias, postal address, phone number, IP address, email, account name No
Personal records (California statute) Name, contact info, education, employment, financial info No
Protected characteristics Gender, date of birth No
Commercial information Transactions, purchase history, payment details No
Biometric information Fingerprints, voiceprints No
Internet / network activity Browsing history, search history, online behavior, interactions No
Geolocation data Device location No
Audio, visual, or similar Images, recordings, video/audio calls No
Professional / employment Job title, work history, professional qualifications No
Education information Student records, directory info No
Inferences from other data User profiles, preference summaries No
Sensitive personal information — No

How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

We don’t store your data, unlike others. Your data security and privacy are our top priorities — we never store any of your information.

Unlike many internet companies that collect and analyze vast amounts of user data, we operate on a fundamentally different principle: transparency and respect for your privacy. We do not collect, save, use, or analyze your identifiers, location information, or any personally identifiable information.

Because we store no data, there is no data processing to protect against. You can use our services knowing your privacy is protected by design.

Will My Information Be Shared With Anyone?

We share only your device and encrypted information with service providers — this is the minimum necessary to deliver effective digital services. We never store, analyze, or sell your personal information.

IceWhale Technology Limited will never engage in any commercial activity involving the sale of user data.

Can I Review, Update, or Delete My Data?

Since we do not store or analyze any of your data, there is no data available for review or deletion. Our no-storage architecture means there is nothing to access, modify, or remove.

Will This Policy Be Updated?

Yes — we update this notice as necessary to stay compliant with relevant laws. Updates are indicated by a revised date at the top of the page. For material changes, we may notify you by posting a prominent notice or sending a direct notification.

Contact Us

If you have questions or comments about this policy, reach us at:

  • Email: support@icewhale.org
  • Post: IceWhale Technology Limited, Room 1318-19, 13/F Hollywood Plaza, 610 Nathan Road, Mongkok, Hong Kong 999077
