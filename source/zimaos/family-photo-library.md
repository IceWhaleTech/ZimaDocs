Every phone in the house holds a piece of the family story. The family photo library brings those pieces together in one place, so the picture your daughter took last week is next to the one you took three years ago, and everyone can find both.

## How It Works

Three steps, one per family member.

1. Each member gets their own ZimaOS account.
2. Everyone installs **[ZimaClient](./zimaclient-install "Install and set up ZimaClient on desktop and mobile for device access")** on their phone and backs up their photos.
3. The backups join the family photo library automatically, while each member's personal library stays private.

![ZimaClient family collection view showing photos from all family members in one library](/images/guides/family-collection-overview.png)

## Browse the Family Collection

The family view shows everyone's photos and videos together, sorted by time. Filter by member to see just one person's pictures, or browse the whole story at once. All three preview modes from **[Photos Preview](./photos-preview "Browse your ZimaOS photos in grid, waterfall, and justified modes")** work here too, so each family member can pick the browsing style they like.

## Add Family Members

1. In ZimaOS settings, create an account for the family member.
2. On their phone, install ZimaClient and sign in with that account.
3. Turn on photo backup. The first backup joins the family collection when it finishes.

![ZimaOS screen for adding a family member account to the photo library](/images/guides/family-add-member.png)

Each member sees the family collection under their own sign-in. Switching between members filters the view, so the kids can find their own pictures quickly.

![Family collection browser switching between members to filter photos by person](/images/guides/family-member-switch.png)

## Everyone's Library Stays Private

The family collection is a shared view, not a shared pool. Each member's personal library remains their own, and only the photos they choose to back up take part. Privacy settings let each member control what appears in the family view.

![Family photo library privacy settings separating personal and shared collections](/images/guides/family-privacy-settings.png)
